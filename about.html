COMPANY PROFILE

Reliable Engineering. Professional Service. Trusted Support.

SAMS Marine Services is a professionally managed marine engineering and technical service company established in 2012.

We specialize in marine compressor servicing and complete overhauling, marine spare parts supply, onboard technical services, workshop repairs, component fabrication and engineering support for the maritime industry.

Our expertise covers inspection, maintenance, repair, troubleshooting and complete overhauling of marine air and refrigeration compressors, along with pump parts fabrication, component repair and workshop reconditioning.

We also support vessel maintenance requirements through the supply of bearings, generator fuel injection parts, oil purifier parts and genuine and OEM-quality spare parts for a wide range of marine machinery.

With experienced marine engineers and skilled technicians, we provide technical assistance onboard vessels as well as through port and workshop facilities.

Our primary objective is to maintain equipment reliability, improve operational performance and minimize vessel downtime.
